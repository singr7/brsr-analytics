Make sense of it

Completeness shows how much of the expected disclosure is present. It does not, by itself, mean the disclosure is specific, evidenced, or high quality.

Why this matters

A complete filing is the starting point. Substance and evidence reveal whether it is decision-useful.

Evidence attached
Every company-level value can link back to the source page, passage, extraction method, and QA status.

Quick check: Does a complete disclosure automatically mean a strong disclosure?

Correct. Completeness measures coverage; substance and evidence are separate dimensions.
